Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Fryers, ranges, and grills dump enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
